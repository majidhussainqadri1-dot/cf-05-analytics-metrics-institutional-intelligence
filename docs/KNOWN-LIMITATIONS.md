# Known Limitations and Evidence Boundaries

The `1.0.0-rc.1` source candidate intentionally does not claim the following without separate evidence:

- operational data-governance staffing or council acceptance;
- real warehouse/provider credentials, regions or contractual approval;
- real cross-file event/metric producers and consumers;
- production scheduler/delivery transport for reports;
- generated export file encryption and object-store delivery;
- real statistical computation engine beyond governed registry, plan and evidence contracts;
- Hostinger staging, browser/accessibility, load, penetration, backup/restore or provider-exit results;
- production or operational acceptance.

These are not silently represented by decorative UI or permissive fallback. Their absence keeps the runtime fail-closed.
